**Beta: testers wanted.** First public release of Pseudo Maker Extras, a CharaStudio plugin that adds maker settings which Studio Pseudo Maker (rikkibalboa) does not have.

**Download**
- Koikatsu / Koikatsu Party: `KK_PseudoMakerExtras.dll`
- Koikatsu Sunshine: `KKS_PseudoMakerExtras.dll`

Put the dll in `BepInEx/plugins`. Requires Studio Pseudo Maker (tested with 1.5.2.0) and ABMX.

**What's in it** (each part turns on when the plugin it works with is installed)
- ABMX bone sliders in the Face, Body and Clothing > Bottom panels, with *Split XYZ scale sliders* and *Side to edit* for left/right pairs
- *Move* button next to *Copy* in the accessory Transfer tab
- Face > Mouth: *Fang* switch, plus EditFangs' length and spacing sliders
- Single Side Clothes switches for clothing and accessories
- ClothingBlendShape sliders in the clothing panels, with Timeline support (click a slider's label)

Tested by the maintainer in KK and KKS CharaStudio, including scene save/load, and without the optional plugins. Please report problems, and successes too, in [Issues](https://github.com/Jotorola-scv/KK-KKS-Plugin-Pack/issues) with the `Features:` line from your log. See the [README](https://github.com/Jotorola-scv/KK-KKS-Plugin-Pack/tree/main/PseudoMakerExtras) for details.
